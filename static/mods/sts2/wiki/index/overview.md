## Keywords

- **Exhaust** removes a card until the end of combat.
- **Sly** rewards discarding a card before the end of your turn.
- StS2 Icon Vulnerable.png **Vulnerable** raises Attack damage taken by 50%.

Cards such as Bash are resolved through the existing Wiki Entity renderer.
